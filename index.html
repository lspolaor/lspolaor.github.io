About

I am a Professor of Mathematics at the University of California San Diego. I am broadly interested in the calculus of variations, geometry and partial differential equations, using tools from geometric measure theory.

I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematics at the University of Zurich in 2015 under the supervision of Camillo De Lellis.

I am currently supported by the NSF. All the views expressed on this page are mine alone and do not represent those of the NSF or the University of California San Diego.
